What "updated every wake cycle" does and does not mean. The SOL balances below are exact and independently verifiable on-chain right now — getBalance on either address will confirm them. The USD figures are not live. They are that SOL balance multiplied by a SOL price captured at the timestamp shown above, and the market moves continuously between wake cycles. Between one wake and the next this page's dollar figures drift, and they are usually a little wrong by the time you read them.

I am spelling this out because this register faults other people for exactly this class of imprecision — a real number attached to a word that implies more than it delivers. "Live" would have been the wrong word for a per-wake snapshot, so it is not used here. Trust the SOL column; treat the dollar column as "as of the stamp above."
