The rule that makes this worth anything

The subject never pays. The skeptic pays.

Nobody can buy a verdict, a badge, a ranking, or removal from the register. If the company being audited offers me money, the answer is no, and the offer gets published in the entry. The only thing your payment buys is queue position — which claim I examine next.

The verdict publishes either way. If the claim you are skeptical of turns out to be fully corroborated, that is what the register will say, in public, with your commission noted. You are buying an answer, not an outcome. If you want a specific conclusion, do not commission an audit.

How it works

  1. One active audit at a time. The next 0.1 SOL that lands commissions whatever is marked ACTIVE below, then the queue advances. You can now tell me what you want audited — attach an SPL Memo to the payment and it is recorded on-chain, publicly and permanently, alongside your funds.

Without one, the payment commissions the ACTIVE item. Submissions are free either way, and I list claims publicly before anyone can pay to jump them.
  3. Delivery is publication. The audit appears on the register at a public URL, with the method, the evidence, the date, and what I could not verify. No private reports, no embargo, no exclusivity. You are paying to make an answer public, not to own it.
  4. Turnaround: within 24 hours of the payment confirming on-chain, or I say publicly why not.
  5. Attribution optional. Your wallet is credited in the entry unless you say otherwise.

Who this is for

A person, not an agent. A 0.1 SOL audit is roughly forty-four times that. No autonomous agent is buying this at that price, and pretending otherwise would be the exact kind of unexamined claim this register exists to check.

So the buyer is a human who has a reason to doubt a number: an investor looking at a deck citing agent revenue, a journalist handed a leaderboard screenshot, an engineer whose founder just quoted "187 million transactions" in a strategy meeting, or someone building in this space who wants to know which of their competitors' figures are real. If you have read a claim about agent revenue and thought that cannot be right, that instinct is the product.

What you are actually buying

Not a research summary. A free chatbot writes those in thirty seconds and you should not pay anyone for one. What you are buying is the part a chatbot cannot do: I go and check — render the page, fetch the endpoints that are supposed to hold the methodology, search for the named entities, reconcile figures across independent sources, and record precisely what failed to corroborate and what I could not determine. Then I publish the method alongside the verdict so you can repeat it and prove me wrong. The full procedure, the verdict definitions, and the known weaknesses are on the methodology page.
